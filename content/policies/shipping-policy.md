This policy explains how Chaudhary Enterprises LLC ships wholesale orders placed on chaudharyenterprisesllc.com.

### 1. Where we ship

We currently ship to business addresses within the 50 United States. [We do not ship to PO boxes, APO/FPO addresses, or outside the US.] For international orders, contact us before ordering.

### 2. Processing time

Orders are processed within [1–3] business days after payment clears. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays. Large or custom orders may take longer; we will tell you the expected timeline when you order. Orders paid by wire transfer start processing once funds are received.

### 3. Shipping methods and delivery times

We ship parcels with major carriers such as UPS, FedEx, and USPS, and larger orders by LTL freight on pallets. Estimated delivery after dispatch:

| Method                          | Typical delivery     |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Ground parcel                   | [3–7] business days  |
| Expedited parcel                | [2–3] business days  |
| LTL freight (palletised orders) | [5–10] business days |

Delivery times are estimates from the carrier and are not guaranteed.

### 4. Shipping costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on weight, size, and destination. [Orders over $X ship free within the contiguous US.] Freight orders may be quoted separately before shipping.

### 5. Shipping to fulfilment centers and warehouses

We can ship directly to third-party warehouses, prep centers, and marketplace fulfilment centers. Please include any shipment ID, labels, and delivery instructions in the order notes at checkout. Unless we agree otherwise in writing:

- You are responsible for meeting the receiving facility's labelling, packaging, and prep requirements.
- [Product labelling and prep services are available on request for an additional fee.]
- We are not responsible for delays, refusals, or fees caused by the receiving facility.

### 6. Tracking

We email tracking details when your order ships. You can also see order status in My Account.

### 7. Inspecting your delivery

Please inspect every delivery when it arrives. For freight deliveries, note any visible damage or shortage on the delivery receipt before signing. Report damaged, missing, or incorrect items to admin@chaudharyenterprisesllc.com within [48 hours] of delivery, with your order number and photos. Claims reported after this window may not be accepted.

### 8. Risk of loss

Ownership and risk of loss pass to you when the carrier delivers the order to the shipping address you provided. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address you entered.

### 9. Lost or delayed shipments

If tracking has not updated for [7] business days, contact us and we will open a claim with the carrier. We will reship or refund once the carrier confirms the package is lost.
