This policy explains how Chaudhary Enterprises LLC ships orders placed on chaudharyenterprisesllc.com.

### 1. Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses within the 50 United States. [We do not ship to PO boxes, APO/FPO addresses, or outside the US.] For international orders, contact us before ordering.

### 2. Processing time

Orders are processed within [1–3] business days after payment clears. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays. Large or custom orders may take longer; we will tell you the expected timeline when you order. Orders paid by wire transfer start processing once funds are received.

### 3. Shipping methods and delivery times

We ship parcels with major carriers such as UPS, FedEx, and USPS. Estimated delivery after dispatch:

| Method           | Typical delivery    |
| ---------------- | ------------------- |
| Ground parcel    | [3–7] business days |
| Expedited parcel | [2–3] business days |

Delivery times are estimates from the carrier and are not guaranteed.

### 4. Shipping costs

Shipping costs are based on weight, size, and destination, and are confirmed in your order confirmation and invoice. [Orders over $X ship free within the contiguous US.]

### 5. Tracking

We email tracking details when your order ships.

### 6. Inspecting your delivery

Please inspect every delivery when it arrives. Report damaged, missing, or incorrect items to admin@chaudharyenterprisesllc.com within [48 hours] of delivery, with your order number and photos. Claims reported after this window may not be accepted.

### 7. Risk of loss

Ownership and risk of loss pass to you when the carrier delivers the order to the shipping address you provided. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address you entered.

### 8. Lost or delayed shipments

If tracking has not updated for [7] business days, contact us and we will open a claim with the carrier. We will reship or refund once the carrier confirms the package is lost.
