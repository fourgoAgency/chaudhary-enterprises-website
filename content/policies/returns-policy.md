All sales are final except in the cases below. This policy explains when we accept returns, how to request one, and how refunds work.

### 1. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If you receive items that are damaged in transit, defective, or not what you ordered, report it within [7] days of delivery. Email admin@chaudharyenterprisesllc.com with:

- Your order number.
- The product name, SKU, and quantity affected.
- Clear photos of the items, the packaging, and the shipping label.

Once we confirm the issue, we will replace the items or refund them, at our choice, and cover the return shipping.

### 2. Shortages

If your delivery is missing items, report it within [48 hours] of delivery.

### 3. Returns for other reasons

Returns of correctly shipped, undamaged products (for example, ordering too many or changing your mind) are accepted only with our written approval and only within [14] days of delivery. If approved:

- Items must be unopened, unused, and in their original, resaleable packaging.
- You pay return shipping.
- A restocking fee of [15]% of the item value applies.

### 4. Items we cannot accept back

For safety and hygiene reasons, the following cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect:

- Food, beverages, and grocery items.
- Dietary supplements, health, and personal care products.
- Beauty and cosmetic products.
- Any item that has been opened, used, relabelled, or had its packaging altered.
- Clearance or specially ordered items marked as final sale.

### 5. How to request a return

1. Email admin@chaudharyenterprisesllc.com with your order number and the reason for the return.
2. Wait for our Return Authorization (RA) number and return instructions. Returns sent without an RA number will be refused.
3. Pack the items securely and write the RA number on the outside of the box.
4. Ship the return to the address we provide, using a trackable service.

### 6. Refunds

We inspect returned items within [5] business days of receiving them. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method, minus any restocking fee and original shipping charges where applicable. Your bank may take [5–10] business days to post the refund.

### 7. Order cancellations

You can cancel an order free of charge before it ships by emailing us with your order number. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled and this Returns policy applies.
